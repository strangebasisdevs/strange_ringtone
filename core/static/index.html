Help

  • Where this server fetches from YouTube, paste a video link under From YouTube, or share the video from the YouTube app to a Make Ringtone Shortcut that opens this page with the link. Only the video's ID reaches the server.
  • Get on this iPhone opens the share sheet. Choose Use as Ringtone, and Settings lists the tone under the name you typed.
  • If the share sheet doesn't offer it, tap Download .m4a or Download .m4r. Open Safari's downloads list (the download icon in the address bar), tap the file, then tap Share and Use as Ringtone.
  • Duplicate Ringtone means iOS already has this tone. Delete the old one, or type a new name and tap Hear final again.
  • To delete a tone, open Settings > Sounds & Haptics > Ringtone (or Text Tone), swipe left on the tone and tap Delete.
  • If you hear nothing, turn off Silent mode.
  • A finished tone is kept on the server for a few minutes only. If a download says it has expired, tap Hear final again.
